Personal Brand

Finance
with Akshay

Finance With Akshay is the personal brand of financial advisor Akshay Gautam, focused on simplifying finance, investing, and wealth creation. Akshay approached us to develop his digital presence into a stronger, more recognisable personal brand. The objective was to create a complete digital ecosystem that presented his expertise professionally, built trust, and converted content into genuine enquiries for his financial services.

Client Akshay Gautam
Industry Financial Services
Service Social Media & Brand Positioning
Channels Instagram, LinkedIn, Meta Ads
The Challenge

What Wasn't WORKING

Despite having strong financial expertise and valuable insights to share, Akshay’s digital presence lacked a clear personal-brand identity, consistent content direction, and a recognisable visual style.

Content was not planned around defined audience needs, shoots were not organised strategically, and there was limited integration between social media, events, lead generation, and his wider online presence.

His seminars, knowledge, and services were not being presented through a structured system across social media, LinkedIn, Google, and the website, making it much harder to build recall, establish authority, and generate quality enquiries.

Objectives

What They Wanted to ACHIEVE

The objective was to build Finance With Akshay into a strong and recognisable personal brand in the finance space. The focus was on creating a consistent brand identity, developing strategic and engaging content, and presenting Akshay’s expertise in a clear, credible, and approachable way across digital platforms.

The overarching goal was to strengthen his authority, improve visibility across Instagram, LinkedIn, Google, and his website, generate quality leads through Meta ads, and create a complete digital presence that could seamlessly turn audience interest into genuine enquiries for his financial services.

Key Performance INDICATORS

  • Content Views & Reach
  • New Audience Acquisition
  • Follower Retention
  • Content Interactions
  • Lead Generation (Enquiries)
Strategy

How We APPROACHED IT

To build Finance With Akshay as a credible and recognisable personal brand, we developed a strategy that combined expert-led content with a consistent visual and communication style. The focus was to simplify financial topics, highlight Akshay’s personality and expertise, and create content that felt informative, relatable, and trustworthy.

We introduced structured content planning, regular photography and video shoots, educational reels, carousels, seminar coverage, and platform-specific content for Instagram and LinkedIn.

Alongside this, we strengthened the complete digital ecosystem through comprehensive website development, Google Business Profile management, and Meta lead-generation campaigns, ensuring every platform worked together to improve visibility, build authority, and generate relevant enquiries.
