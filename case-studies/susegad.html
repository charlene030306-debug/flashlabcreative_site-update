Perfume & Fragrance

Susegad
The Fragrance of Goa

Susegad The Fragrance of Goa is a Goan perfume brand that sells perfumes, candles, attars, air fresheners, and other fragrance-based products. The brand approached us with the goal of increasing engagement, building stronger brand awareness, and showcasing that they are one of the only true Goan Perfume Brands. They wanted their digital presence to feel more engaging, product-focused, and trend-led while authentically highlighting their full range of products.

Client Susegad
Industry Perfume & Lifestyle
Service Social Media Management
Channels Instagram, Meta Ads
The Challenge

What Wasn't WORKING

As a product-based brand, Susegad required a very different content approach compared to service or restaurant-based clients. One of the biggest challenges was making the account engaging and trending while consistently showcasing static products in a way that felt fresh and interesting.

The account lacked a proper feed structure, Instagram highlights were missing, and the overall page did not have a consistent visual presentation. Furthermore, reels were not performing well prior to our intervention.

Another major hurdle was effectively communicating the uniqueness of the brand—specifically establishing their identity as a true Goan perfume manufacturer. The page required regular product-focused content, offer promotions, and an engagement strategy to break through the noise.

Objectives

What They Wanted to ACHIEVE

The primary objective was to increase brand awareness and engagement for Susegad The Fragrance of Goa.

The client wanted to seamlessly showcase their products, cover their full product range (perfumes, attars, candles, etc.), promote offers through well-designed posters, and create a highly active social media presence. A key underlying objective was to solidify their identity as a Goan perfume brand and expand their footprint through influencer collaborations and targeted paid ads.

Key Performance INDICATORS

  • Follower growth & retention
  • Overall profile engagement
  • Content reach (Reels & Posts)
  • Product awareness metrics
  • Offer promotion performance
  • Influencer collaboration ROI
  • Paid campaign performance
Strategy

How We APPROACHED IT

We developed a robust content strategy focused purely on showcasing Susegad’s product range in a more engaging, trend-led, and visually appealing way.

The visual narrative highlighted their perfumes, candles, attars, and air fresheners while maintaining a consistent and elevated brand presence. We introduced offer-based posters and reels to strategically promote ongoing deals and drive immediate customer interest.

To make the page feel alive and culturally relevant, we incorporated trending audio formats and product-led creatives. Influencer collaborations were systematically included to leverage external audiences, driving stronger reach, credibility, and widespread product awareness across Goa and beyond.

Finally, paid ads were deployed to push high-performing organic content, gaining followers, building brand awareness, and lifting overall profile engagement.
