Case Studies Restaurant & Hospitality Babs Hideout
Restaurant & Hospitality

Babs
Hideout

Babs Hideout is a restaurant located in Benaulim, Goa, known for its relaxed ambience, quality food, cocktails, and vibrant dining experience. We were entrusted with managing the brand’s complete social media presence, helping strengthen its digital identity through strategic content creation, high-quality photography, and consistent online engagement.

Client Babs Hideout
Industry Restaurant & Hospitality
Service Social Media Management & Ads
Channels Instagram, Meta Ads
The Challenge

What Wasn't WORKING

Babs Hideout had the opportunity to build a more structured and engaging digital presence. While the restaurant was well-known locally for its food, ambience, cocktails, and customer experience, these highlights were not being showcased consistently across social media.

The Instagram profile lacked a clear content direction, visual consistency, and organised highlights. There was an urgent need for regular promotional creatives, engaging reels, high-quality photography, and a stronger brand identity across all content formats.

Additionally, the brand needed to proactively improve its online visibility, attract both tourists and local audiences, increase organic reach, and strengthen overall customer engagement through creative storytelling.

Objectives

What They Wanted to ACHIEVE

The primary objective was to strengthen Babs Hideout’s digital presence by creating a consistent and engaging social media strategy that reflected the restaurant’s ambience, food, cocktails, and overall dining experience.

The focus was on building a strong brand identity through high-quality photography, reels, and graphic creatives that resonated with both tourists and local audiences. Additional goals included establishing Babs Hideout as a recognised dining destination in South Goa by consistently showcasing its offerings, promoting special events, and creating meaningful connections through performance-driven social media marketing.

Key Performance INDICATORS

  • Follower growth
  • Engagement rate
  • Reel performance
  • Reach and impressions
  • Profile visits
  • Story interactions

Strategy

How We APPROACHED IT

We developed and implemented a comprehensive social media strategy focused on consistency, creativity, and audience engagement. The content plan was meticulously designed to showcase Babs Hideout’s food, cocktails, ambience, customer experiences, and special events through a mix of high-quality photography, videography, reels, stories, and graphic creatives.

Regular content shoots ensured a steady flow of fresh visuals, while promotional campaigns and seasonal content kept the audience engaged throughout the year. We also optimised the Instagram profile with organised highlights and maintained a consistent visual identity to strengthen the brand’s online presence.

To expand the restaurant’s reach, we incorporated influencer collaborations and targeted Meta ad campaigns alongside our organic content output. Performance was continuously monitored to refine the content strategy, improve engagement, and maximise reach, allowing us to attract both local customers and tourists effectively.

Strategic Outcomes

  • High Performing Content: Food reels, event content, live music nights, ambience videos, and experience-based reels consistently generated strong engagement and increased content shareability.
  • Increased Reach & Engagement: Regular content, seasonal campaigns, and live music promotions contributed to sustained growth in views, interactions, and overall audience engagement.
  • Stronger Brand Visibility: Influencer collaborations, Meta ad campaigns, and co-branded content helped expand the brand's reach and introduce it to new audiences.
  • Improved Event Awareness: Consistent promotion of live music events helped maintain customer interest and strengthen the brand's entertainment identity.
  • Enhanced Digital Experience: Organised highlights, an improved profile structure, and consistent creative content made it easier for customers to explore the brand and stay updated with upcoming events.
